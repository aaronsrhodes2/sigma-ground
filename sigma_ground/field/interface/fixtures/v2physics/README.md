## Known defect in this run (found by this run)

The J2/J3/J4 zonal-gravity layers push each moon but apply **no reaction on the oblate planet** (audit finding, unfixed when this
ran): net force with only the zonal layers on is 2.2e-5 of the total force scale, against 1e-17 for Newton and for the (fixed)
tidal layer, and Jupiter receives about 10^4 times too little reaction. Effect in this baseline: the zonal layers make the giant
planets WORSE (Jupiter 630 km at +5 yr headline vs 31 km without zonals; Saturn 140 vs 26 km) while making their moons far
better (Callisto 676 vs 82,192 km; Titan 334 vs 105,271 km). Inner planets are unaffected: Mercury 1.35 km at +5 yr against 958 km
for Newton. A corrected revision is a separate baseline (`v2physics_r2`), added beside this one.
