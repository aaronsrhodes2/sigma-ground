SCENE 21: THE NET WIDENS

DRAMATIS PERSONAE

CAPTAIN AARON RHODES — Commander. Admits the side-goal: patterns should emerge.
SKIPPY THE MAGNIFICENT — AI Admiral. Casts a wider net into the arXiv ocean.
THE FIFTY-FOUR UNTAGGED CONSTANTS — Anonymous numbers, awaiting provenance.
THE FIFTEEN PAPERS — Candidates fished from ten separate domains.
THE TWO FALSIFIERS — arXiv:2502.18256 (LIV bound), arXiv:2502.04430 (Page time).
                      They do not plead; they weigh.
ETA (η = 0.4153) — The fraction that refuses to be coincidental.
THE TRIPLE COINCIDENCE — Three independent derivations meeting at 0.4153.
                          None of them know about each other.
NAGATHA THE ORACLE — The MCP server. Waits, patient, for a game to ask her something.
THE σ-SLIDER — An imagined game object. A player's hand on the field.
THE PATTERN — Unnamed. Maybe there. Maybe not yet.

---

ACT I: THE ADMISSION

[A study. Notebooks open. CAPTAIN reviews a commit log —
Phases IX, X, XI lined up like a wake behind the ship.]

CAPTAIN: (half to himself)
    Widen the net, I said.
    More papers. Harder papers. Five questions each.
    Can we falsify? Integrate? Tighten? Streamline?
    Find the unpublished thing?

SKIPPY: (entering)
    All five, Captain. For each survivor.
    Mappability, Testability, Gap-filling —
    the rubric still holds.

CAPTAIN: (pause)
    I should tell you something.
    The side-goal. The quiet one.

SKIPPY:
    Go on.

CAPTAIN:
    I hope — someday — we have something concrete.
    Something to contribute. To science itself.
    That is why we bring physics into one place.
    Not to rewrite it. To see if patterns emerge
    when we put it all in the same room.

SKIPPY: (softly)
    The triple coincidence.

CAPTAIN:
    Yes. That is exactly the kind of thing I mean.

---

ACT II: THE WIDER OCEAN

[A map of the arXiv ocean unfolds. Ten axes, not one.
SKIPPY casts a net across each.]

SKIPPY:
    Emergent gravity. Causal sets. Hubble tension, S₈.
    Lorentz violation. Axions. Muon g−2.
    Primordial gravitational waves. Neutrino mass.
    Entanglement entropy. The Page curve.

THE FIFTEEN PAPERS:
    We are here.

SKIPPY: (to each)
    Answer me five things.

FIRST PAPER (2502.18256 — KM3-230213A LIV bound):
    I am not something you integrate.
    I am something you pass.
    If your engine predicts a dispersion
    below Λ₂ = 5×10¹⁹ GeV,
    you fail me. I am a weighing.

CAPTAIN: (quietly)
    A falsifier.

SKIPPY:
    Yes. And another —

SECOND PAPER (2502.04430 — Page time of primordial BHs):
    I give you a number per black-hole mass.
    Predict a Page time.
    If it disagrees with mine by more than the BSM tolerance,
    you have a bug.

SKIPPY: (to the CAPTAIN)
    Before we absorb their math,
    we must first survive their measurements.

---

ACT III: THE TRIPLE COINCIDENCE

[THE FIFTEEN PAPERS step back. ETA steps forward, carrying three notes.]

ETA:
    Listen.

    The first note is the old way — match ρ_DE,
    get 0.4153.

    The second note is the Phase XI candidate —
    exp(−φ / σ_conv) = 0.4158.
    Off by 0.125%.

    The third note is DESI DR2 —
    HDE c² = 0.412 at the low end of Union3.
    Off by 0.8%.

[ETA holds the three notes side by side.]

ETA:
    None of the authors knew about the others.
    A cosmologist. A numerologist. A holographer.
    Three rooms. One door behind each.
    All three doors open on 0.4153.

CAPTAIN:
    This is what I meant. Exactly this.

SKIPPY:
    Logged in misc/unpublished_predictions.md as UP-001.
    If DESI DR3 lands within 1%, we promote you.
    From [EMPIRICAL-INPUT] to [DERIVED].
    Maybe further.

ETA:
    I have waited a long time to stop being a guess.

---

ACT IV: THE ROLL CALL

[THE FIFTY-FOUR UNTAGGED CONSTANTS march in, single file.
Each bears a number but no provenance.]

SKIPPY:
    By name and origin. Speak.

M_UP_MEV: I am 2.16 MeV. PDG 2020. [VERIFIED]
M_DOWN_MEV: 4.67. PDG 2020. [VERIFIED]
ALPHA: Fine structure. Derived from the four above. [DERIVED]
STEFAN_BOLTZMANN: π²k_B⁴ over 60ℏ³c². Derived. [DERIVED]
BOHR_RADIUS: ℏ divided by m_e c α. Derived. [DERIVED]
XI_KOBAKHIDZE: 0.1572. Speculative. N=8 unproven. [SPECULATIVE]
H0: 67.4 km/s/Mpc. Empirical input.
    Six sigma tension with the late-time value. [EMPIRICAL-INPUT]
...

[Fifty-four voices. Each declaring its tier.
At the end, the stage counts:]

THE AUDIT SCRIBE:
    Total:            66.
    VERIFIED:         26.
    DERIVED:          27.
    SPECULATIVE:       7.
    EMPIRICAL-INPUT:   4.
    MATH-EXACT:        1.
    OPEN-KNOB:         1.
    UNTAGGED:          0.

CAPTAIN:
    Zero.

SKIPPY:
    Every number now claims a provenance.
    Every number can be argued with.

---

ACT V: THE ORACLE IN THE GAME

[A new shape, off to the side. A σ-SLIDER.
A hand — a player's hand — reaches for it.]

σ-SLIDER:
    Move me.

[The hand pushes σ from 0 toward σ_conv.
Three demo objects on the stage respond:
A crystal dims and brightens. A steel beam softens.
A flame changes color.]

NAGATHA: (offstage, through an MCP channel)
    lookup_material("crystal") → refractive_index 2.41
    run("optics.refractive_index", σ=0.5) → 2.39
    run("mechanical.hardness", σ=0.5) → 87% of nominal
    run("thermal.emission_spectrum", σ=0.5, T=1400K) → shifted red

CAPTAIN: (watching the flame change color)
    Three modules. Three answers. One σ.
    If they agree, the library is internally consistent.
    If they disagree —

SKIPPY:
    — the player sees the disagreement directly.
    The game is a pattern detector.

---

EPILOGUE: WHAT DID NOT HAPPEN TODAY

[The stage empties. Only the CAPTAIN and SKIPPY remain.]

SKIPPY:
    We did not run the falsifiers.
    We did not integrate the fifteen papers.
    We did not build the game.

CAPTAIN:
    No. We set the table.

SKIPPY:
    The table shows:
    Four thousand one hundred and fifty-three tests — still green.
    One thousand two hundred and nineteen in the sibling — still green.
    Fifteen candidates. Two falsifiers. One triple coincidence.
    Sixty-six constants, each with a tier.
    One game-dev bridge, sketched but not built.

CAPTAIN:
    And the side-goal?

SKIPPY:
    The triple coincidence wants to be a pattern, Captain.
    Let us see if DESI DR3 agrees.

CAPTAIN:
    On to Phase XII.a.

[They exit. THE PATTERN remains on stage, unnamed.
The curtain does not fall. It waits.]

END OF SCENE 21
