# Confidence-Tier Audit — 2026-04-17 (Phase XII)

## Purpose

Apply the `[SPECULATIVE] / [THEORETICAL] / [VERIFIED]` confidence-rating convention
**throughout the existing codebase**, not just on new additions. Previously the
convention was limited to Tier-1 arXiv imports (Phases IX–XI); this pass extends it to
every module-level constant in `constants.py` across both checkouts, and introduces two
new tiers to cover cases the three-tier scheme could not express cleanly.

## Extended tier vocabulary (adopted 2026-04-17)

- **`[VERIFIED]`** — CODATA / PDG / NIST / SI-exact, or reproduces a published
  measurement within its stated tolerance. Examples: `G`, `C`, `HBAR`, `M_UP_MEV`,
  `R0_FM`.
- **`[DERIVED]`** — computed inline from `[VERIFIED]` or `[EMPIRICAL-INPUT]` constants
  via an explicit formula in this file, with no new free parameters. Examples:
  `ALPHA`, `STEFAN_BOLTZMANN`, `SIGMA_CONV`, `THETA_ENTANGLEMENT_PER_DIM`.
- **`[THEORETICAL]`** — first-principles grounding for an existing formula; not yet
  empirically discriminated but reduces the degree of freedom. Examples (planned):
  `gamma_csl_linear` once confirmed by Phase H intensity scan.
- **`[SPECULATIVE]`** — imported from a single paper, not yet pipeline-confirmed.
  Examples: `XI_KOBAKHIDZE`, `C_HDE_UNION3`, `ETA_FORMULA`.
- **`[EMPIRICAL-INPUT]`** *(new tier)* — phenomenological fit, not a direct
  measurement and not derived in-engine. Examples: `XI`, `ETA`, `GAMMA`, `H0`.
  These are the highest-leverage knobs; promotion to `[VERIFIED]` or `[DERIVED]`
  via new evidence is the point of future integration work.
- **`[MATH-EXACT]`** *(new tier)* — pure mathematical identity (irrational constant
  with exact closed form). Example: `PHI = (1+√5)/2`.
- **`[OPEN-KNOB]`** *(new tier)* — intentionally unfixed, resolved empirically
  downstream. Example: `GAMMA_COHERENCE_DEFAULT = 1.0`.

## Notable observations from this audit

1. **η is `[EMPIRICAL-INPUT]`, not `[DERIVED]`.** The existing block comment called it
   DERIVED from the ρ_DE constraint, but "derived from" in the engine's formal sense
   means "computed inline from other constants in this file." η = 0.4153 is hard-coded,
   with the *motivation* being a ρ_DE match. Correct tier is `[EMPIRICAL-INPUT]` until
   `ETA_FORMULA = exp(−φ/σ_conv)` is confirmed and promoted to `[DERIVED]`.
2. **XI = 0.1582 is similarly `[EMPIRICAL-INPUT]`.** Its comment says "INPUT" — that
   matches. Kobakhidze N=8 (arXiv:2410.22412) is the standing candidate derivation at
   0.1572, which would promote XI to `[DERIVED]` if the N=8 identification with our
   σ-field structure is established.
3. **LAMBDA_QCD_MEV = 217 is `[VERIFIED]` (PDG) but should be re-checked against the
   Muon g−2 Theory Initiative 2025 white paper.** That is the highest-leverage free
   precision test in Batch 3.
4. **H0 = 67.4 km/s/Mpc is `[EMPIRICAL-INPUT]`.** With DESI 2025 reporting >6σ tension
   (arXiv:2512.07281), the tag signals "the value may move" — future Phase work should
   evaluate both 67.4 (early-universe) and ~73 (late-universe) values when the
   resulting numbers matter.
5. **GAMMA = 2.035** (Planck-derived spectral index) deserves a provenance note —
   currently tagged `[EMPIRICAL-INPUT]`. Separately, `GAMMA_COHERENCE_DEFAULT = 1.0`
   was reclassified `[OPEN-KNOB]` to make clear it is intentionally unfixed, not an
   oversight.

## Out of scope for this pass

- Formula-level tagging inside `sigma_ground/field/interface/*.py` (~78 modules,
  hundreds of top-level formulas). Tracked as a Phase XIII follow-on. The new
  `confidence_summary()` helper is deliberately written to be extended to walk
  interface modules in a future pass — only the regex needs to change.
- Formula-level tagging in `sigma_ground/inventory/` and `sigma_ground/dynamics/`.
  Same Phase XIII scope.
- Retiring the older `audit.py` origin vocabulary (`CORE`, `MEASURED`,
  `FIRST_PRINCIPLES`, `EXTERNAL`, `TAUTOLOGICAL`, `DERIVED`) — deliberately kept
  alongside the new tier vocabulary, since the formula-audit registry uses the old
  vocabulary and the constants-audit uses the new. Future consolidation: a single
  vocabulary with machine-readable cross-references.
