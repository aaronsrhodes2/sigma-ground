## 1. What is new in AdS/CFT and de Sitter holography (dS/CFT, static-patch holography, cosmological horizons, holographic dark energy cutoffs), and was holographic dark energy fitted against DESI?

### Takeaway
Formal de Sitter holography moved forward on a "worldline / observer" picture: a static-patch density of states from a dS3 ↔ complex-Liouville-string ↔ double-scaled SYK chain, plus stretched-horizon complexity. None of this makes a lab-checkable prediction. Holographic dark energy (HDE) was fitted to DESI DR2 heavily. The original HDE and Ricci DE are reported excluded. Hubble-cutoff and particle-horizon variants are killed once CMB priors are added. Future-event-horizon, Granda–Oliveros (GO), Barrow, Viaggiu and generalized-entropy variants survive, but they are statistically indistinguishable from ΛCDM (ΔAIC ≈ 0–2, |Δln Z| < 1). DESI's own July 2026 Lyα full-shape result also moved toward ΛCDM.

### Cited Findings

**De Sitter / static-patch holography (THEORY)**
- *An observer's quantization of 3d de Sitter*, by A. Blommaert, D. Tietto and H. Verlinde. arXiv:2606.26241 [hep-th], submitted 24 Jun 2026 (IN). They propose a definition and exact calculation of the **density of states of the dS static patch**. It is a sum over an SL(2,ℤ) family of Euclidean no-boundary "Kerr-lens" spacetimes sourced by a line defect, which in Lorentzian time is an observer's worldline. The calculation uses a duality between dS₃ gravity and two copies of the complex Liouville string (ℂLS). The crosscap amplitudes match the semiclassical gravity prediction. The simplest case is dual to two copies of the GΣ effective theory of double-scaled SYK living on the observer's worldline, which the authors present as a "microscopic worldline hologram of 3d de Sitter." — [arXiv:2606.26241](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.26241)

Timelike-subregion volume complexity in pure dS grows exponentially at short durations and "hyperfast" near a maximal duration. In Schwarzschild–de Sitter with the stretched horizon near the black-hole horizon, the hyperfast growth is replaced by nonlinear growth. ### Inferences
- Hook (standard HDE definition, Li 2004 — [hep-th/0403127](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0403127)): ρ_de = 3c²M_p²L⁻² with M_p² = 1/(8πG). For the future-event-horizon cutoff L = R_eh = a∫_t^∞ dt'/a, the EoS is w = −1/3 − (2/3)√Ω_de / c. These formulas come from that background paper, as I recall them, not from the 2026 fits.
- Hook (Hubble cutoff, my derivation): with L = 1/H and c = 1, ρ_de = 3M_p²H² equals the critical density exactly. An observed Ω_de ≈ 0.69 therefore requires c² ≈ 0.69 in the naive Hubble-cutoff model. This variant is the one the 2026 fits reject once CMB priors are included (2608.09379). **[HUBBLE-BOUND]** Any sigma-ground model that saturates a holographic bound exactly at R_H = c/H is in the observationally disfavoured class unless it carries extra dynamics, such as the Ḧ/H or Ḣ terms of the GO family.
- Hook (GO cutoff, as defined in the GO literature, e.g. [arXiv:2311.12409](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2311.12409)): ρ_de = 3M_p²(αH² + βḢ). 2610.11616 adds the extensions +γH⁻² and +γḦ/H. These are implementable as a closed ODE system in H(z).
- Hook (Barrow): area-law deformation S ∝ A^{1+Δ/2}, giving ρ_de ∝ L^{Δ−2}. The DESI-era data keep Δ consistent with 0 (2610.11616, 2608.23585). Treat Δ = 0, the pure Bekenstein–Hawking area law, as the data-preferred value.
- Net: no HDE variant beats ΛCDM decisively on DESI DR2. Original HDE and RDE are claimed excluded. A Python engine could reproduce these fits with a background-only H(z) integrator plus the DESI DR2 BAO D_M/r_d and D_H/r_d points.

## 2. What is new on entanglement and spacetime emergence (ER=EPR, Ryu–Takayanagi, islands, Page curve, QES), and are there quantum-simulator realizations (wormhole teleportation, SYK)?

### Takeaway
Theory activity was incremental: island information localization via the covariant entropy bound, mutual-information structure after the Page time, and a revised "Realization of ER=EPR." On hardware, small-N SYK wormhole-inspired teleportation and Hayden–Preskill recovery continue on NISQ devices, but only **qualitative** signatures (sign-dependent asymmetry) survive the noise. I found no Google or IBM holography/wormhole announcement in the window. Google's 2026 news was a Willow early-access programme.

### Cited Findings
- *Where in the Island is the Information?*, by T. Banks. arXiv:2610.02406, 1 Oct 2026 (IN; THEORY). For large evaporating AdS black holes, Banks argues that **all quantum information in the island is concentrated in a tiny spacelike region very close to the horizon at the Page time**. The first argument applies the covariant entropy bound (CEB) to causal diamonds of geodesics starting in the would-be island region: all points except a stretched-horizon region have entropy bounds that are "tiny fractions of the black hole entropy." The second argument uses tensor-network RG of the CFT. — [arXiv:2610.02406](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02406)
- *New insights on mutual information in the island approach to the Page curve*, by A. Roy Chowdhury, S. Paul and S. Gangopadhyay. arXiv:2607.04706, Jul 2026 (IN; THEORY). For the eternal black hole, I(B₊:B₋) vanishes at the scrambling time, which is what yields the correct Page curve. At the same observer time, I(𝓘:R) between island and radiation **diverges**. — [arXiv:2607.04706](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.04706)
- *Realization of "ER=EPR"*, arXiv:2411.18485, **v3 dated 29 Jun 2026** (a revision of a 2024 preprint; THEORY). It aims to derive the Einstein–Rosen bridge from entanglement in the thermofield-double CFT. It argues that the wormhole's Bekenstein–Hawking entropy equals an entanglement entropy between subsystems, and identifies the disjoint-interval RT geodesic in the ER bridge as the entanglement-wedge cross-section. — [arXiv:2411.18485v3](https://arxiv.org/html/2411.18485v3)
- *Hayden–Preskill recovery at finite temperature on a quantum processor: dynamics and initial state from the SYK model*, by J. Bang, M. Byun, K. Cho, K.-Y. Kim and H. Lee. arXiv:2607.28486, 30 Jul 2026 (IN; THEORY plus quantum-processor protocol). It uses a SWAP gate so the scrambler and the initial thermofield state are related, at finite temperature. **Both the post-selection probability and the conditional fidelity scale ∝ temperature**, reflecting reduced initial entanglement at low T. Late-time analytic estimates assume uniform operator spreading and agree with numerics. — [arXiv:2607.28486](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28486)
- *Quantum simulation of traversable-wormhole-inspired quantum teleportation in a chaotic binary sparse SYK model*, by M. Byun, K.-Y. Kim and H. Lee. arXiv:2604.10090, Apr 2026 (BACKGROUND; EXPERIMENT on a quantum processor). It implements the traversable-wormhole protocol with a **binary sparse N = 8 SYK** Hamiltonian, which cuts circuit depth. NISQ noise "precludes perfect quantitative agreement" with exact numerics, but the key qualitative signature, a **sign-dependent asymmetry** of the mutual-information teleportation signal, is observed. — [arXiv:2604.10090](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.10090)
- Related 2026 items (snippets only): PT-symmetric non-Hermitian deformation of minimal SYK teleportation, arXiv:2601.17688, which reports a "purification" effect in the broken phase — [awesomepapers summary](https://awesomepapers.io/quantum-computing/papers/2601.17688); a trapped-ion sparse-SYK simulation by Granet et al. in npj Quantum Information, mentioned in search results but not verified. ### Inferences
- Hook (SYK, standard model): H = Σ_{i<j<k<l} J_{ijkl} χ_iχ_jχ_kχ_l with ⟨J²⟩ = 3!J²/N³. At N = 8 Majoranas the Hilbert space is 2^{N/2} = 16-dimensional, so a Python engine can exactly diagonalize it and reproduce (a) the sign-dependent asymmetry of the wormhole-teleportation mutual information (2604.10090) and (b) the linear-in-T scaling of Hayden–Preskill post-selection probability and fidelity (2607.28486). These are cheap, honest benchmarks.
- Hook (RT / island): S(A) = Area(γ_A)/4G_N; S_gen = Area(∂I)/4G + S_matter(R ∪ I), extremized then minimized. Banks' CEB argument reduces to checking S ≤ A/4G on light-sheets for causal diamonds near the horizon. That can be implemented for a Schwarzschild–AdS background.

The island picture of entanglement between causally disconnected regions is the nearest formal analogue to an "electromagnetically incommensurable pocket," but I found no paper making that connection, and the link is my speculation.

## 3. What happened on tabletop tests of quantum gravity: gravitationally induced entanglement (BMV), levitated masses, short-distance Newton's law (Eöt-Wash, sub-mm fifth forces), and the equivalence principle (MICROSCOPE follow-ups)?

### Takeaway
**No gravitationally-induced-entanglement experiment has been performed. Everything in the window on GIE is theory or proposal.** That covers geometry optimization, constrained-dynamics pendula, atom-interferometer variants, and critiques of the witness logic. Hardware is creeping toward the regime: a 1-mg torsion pendulum cooled to 240 μK, and a stopped-light torsion sensor resolving 3.02 × 10⁻¹⁶ N from a mm-sized mass. The one new experimental equivalence-principle bound in the window is the **China Space Station dual-Rb atom-interferometer WEP test, η = (−2.7 ± 4.7) × 10⁻⁷**. It is far weaker than MICROSCOPE (≈10⁻¹⁵) but is a first in orbit. No new Eöt-Wash or sub-mm inverse-square-law limit was found in the window; a May 2026 review compiles the existing limits.

### Inferences
- Hook (Unruh, standard): T_U = ħa / (2π c k_B), so a ≈ 2.47 × 10²⁰ m/s² per kelvin (my arithmetic, consistent with 2606.21019's "10²⁰").
- Hook (analogue Hawking, standard): T_H = ħκ / (2π k_B) with κ the surface gravity, or the velocity gradient at the horizon in fluid analogues. In the fibre system the authors use the NRR as a "thermometer": log(normalized Hawking / NRR intensity) is linear in frequency, with a slope ∝ 1/T. The engine can fit that slope.
- Hook (Hawking temperature of a mass M, standard): T = ħc³ / (8πGMk_B) ≈ 1.23 × 10²³ K·kg / M. The r_s = R_H universe mass of ~9.2 × 10⁵² kg gives ~1.3 × 10⁻³⁰ K (§5).
- None of these experiments addresses a "space cavitation" pocket. The analogue horizons are optical or acoustic, so effective metrics for one field, not spacetime. That is consistent with treating any "EM-incommensurable pocket" in sigma-ground as speculative and without experimental analogue (my inference).
