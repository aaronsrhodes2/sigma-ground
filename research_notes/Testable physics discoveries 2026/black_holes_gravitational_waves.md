## Q1. What is new from LIGO-Virgo-KAGRA (catalogs, O5 status, GW250114, area theorem, ringdown/Kerr, GW231123, hierarchical & eccentric mergers, GR tests)?

### Takeaway
The O4 catalogs are now public through O4b: GWTC-5.0 (26 May 2026) brings the total to 390 candidates, and inside the window LVK released the GWTC-5.0 GR-test paper (no deviation from GR, 168 events) while several groups (including LVK-listed papers) pushed GW250114's ringdown into the nonlinear regime (quadratic QNMs at ~3σ, δf220 constrained to ~2%). Population results now show a BH mass-spectrum peak at ~10 M☉, a slope break at ~35–38 M☉, and a high-spin (χ≈0.7) hierarchical-merger subpopulation switching on near ~45 M☉ — identified with the lower edge of the pair-instability gap (44.3 +5.9/−3.5 M☉).

### Inferences
- *(derived)* **Area-theorem hook for GW250114**: with Kerr area A = 8π(GM/c²)²(1+√(1−χ²)) and median values (χ₁,₂ ≈ 0, M_f = 62.7, χ_f = 0.68): A_i ≈ 1.089×10⁵ (GM☉/c²)² ≈ 2.37×10⁵ km², A_f ≈ 1.712×10⁵ (GM☉/c²)² ≈ 3.73×10⁵ km², so **A_f/A_i ≈ 1.57 (ΔA/A ≈ +0.57)**. Radiated energy ≈ 3.1 M☉c² ≈ 4.7% of initial mass, well below the 29% Hawking bound for non-spinning inputs. A sigma-ground merger routine should reproduce ΔA/A ≈ 0.57 and E_rad/M ≈ 0.05 for these inputs.
- *(derived)* **Ringdown hook**: the standard Kerr fit (Berti, Cardoso & Will 2006 — from my background knowledge, not fetched here) Mω₂₂₀ = 1.5251 − 1.1568(1−χ)^0.1292, Q₂₂₀ = 0.7 + 1.4187(1−χ)^−0.4990, τ = Q/(πf), evaluated at detector-frame M_z = t_Mf/t☉ = 0.337 ms/4.926 µs ≈ 68.4 M☉ and χ = 0.68, gives **f₂₂₀ ≈ 248.7 Hz, τ₂₂₀ ≈ 4.10 ms**, matching the measured 247–252 Hz and 4.1 ms. The 221 fit (F = 1.3673 − 1.0260(1−χ)^0.1628, Q = 0.1 + 0.5436(1−χ)^−0.4731) gives f₂₂₁ ≈ 243 Hz, τ₂₂₁ ≈ 1.35 ms (measured f₂₂₁ = 249 +8/−9 Hz). Hawking temperature of the 62.7 M☉ remnant ≈ 1.0×10⁻⁹ K.
- **Mass thresholds for the "bond-failure layer" collapse sequence**: observations now separate "first-generation" stellar collapse (BHs ≲ 40–45 M☉, peak ~10 M☉) from hierarchical assembly (≥ 45 M☉, χ ~ 0.7). A model that yields stellar-collapse BHs inside 45–130 M☉ without a merger history conflicts with these data, unless it reproduces the GW231123-style high-spin route (rotation + magnetic fields, or eccentric hierarchical capture).
- **Exterior-geometry constraint on any σ-field / "space cavitation" interior**: whatever sigma-ground puts inside the horizon, the exterior QNM spectrum must stay Kerr to |δf₂₂₀| ≲ 0.02–0.03 and |δτ₂₂₀| ≲ 0.1 (GW250114). The area must increase, by ≈57% for GW250114-like inputs. Quadratic QNMs at the GR-predicted coupling are a new nonlinear check.

## Q2. What did pulsar timing arrays report on the nanohertz background (amplitude, spectral index, SMBHB vs cosmological)?

### Takeaway
No PTA has reached 5σ, and neither a NANOGrav 20-yr nor an IPTA DR3 GWB result appeared in the window. NANOGrav's re-analysis of the 15-yr data with custom chromatic noise models (June 2026) *lowers* the amplitude to A = 2.1 +0.6/−0.5 ×10⁻¹⁵ (γ = 13/3 fixed), steepens γ to 3.5 +0.7/−0.6, and raises the HD evidence (BF 1571, 3.32σ). That moves the signal toward the SMBHB expectation (γ = 13/3, A ≲ 1.9×10⁻¹⁵).

### Inferences
- *(derived)* **PTA hook**: h_c(f) = A (f/1 yr⁻¹)^((3−γ)/2) and h²Ω_GW(f) = (2π²/3H₁₀₀²) f² h_c(f)², with H₁₀₀ = 100 km/s/Mpc. A = 2.1×10⁻¹⁵ gives h²Ω_GW(1/yr) ≈ 2.8×10⁻⁹; A = 2.4×10⁻¹⁵ gives ≈ 3.6×10⁻⁹. γ = 13/3 corresponds to circular, GW-driven SMBHB inspirals (h_c ∝ f^(−2/3)).
- The 2026 trend (lower A, γ closer to 13/3, astrophysical ceiling A ≲ 1.9×10⁻¹⁵) eases the "too loud for SMBHBs" tension and weakens the need for cosmological sources. Cosmological-source fits are "prior dominated" and shifted only marginally.

## Q7. What is new in stellar collapse physics (CCSN → BH thresholds, failed supernovae, TOV maximum mass, lower mass gap, NICER radii)?

### Takeaway
Direct BH formation by a "failed supernova" now has its best case: M31-2014-DS1 (Science, Feb 2026). A ≈13 M☉ (ZAMS) star stripped to ≈5 M☉ faded ≳10⁴× in optical and to ~7–8% of its bolometric luminosity, consistent with ~98% fallback into a ≈5 M☉ BH after only a ~10⁴⁶–10⁴⁸ erg shock; a stellar-merger interpretation is still argued. The neutron-star ceiling is anchored at M_TOV ≈ 2.2–2.3 M☉ (J0952−0607 at 2.35 ± 0.11 M☉ gives M_TOV > 2.27 M☉ at 1σ). A new NICER radius for 1.937 M☉ PSR J1614−2230 (Aug 31, 2026) is compact (R_eq = 10.06 +1.25/−0.87 km). The GW-inferred PISN-gap lower edge is ≈44 M☉.
